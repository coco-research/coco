It doesn't answer. It deliberates.

Ask a hard question and CoCo convenes the experts who actually disagree about it. They argue in rounds, positions move, and dissent is recorded by name rather than averaged away. You get the reasoning, the split, and who held which line.

Ninety seconds. Then it's yours.

One command. It clones, shows you the exact commit you're about to run, and waits for you to say yes.

Step one

Run it

bash <(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/coco-research/coco/main/bin/coco-bootstrap.sh)

Step two

Confirm the commit

CoCo prints the hash, date and message, then pauses. Nothing executes until you approve it.

Step three

Convene the board

/SI-Decide "should we migrate to Postgres?"
