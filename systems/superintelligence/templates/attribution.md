## Persona attribution (mandatory)

Wherever a persona's view appears, label it exactly, next to that persona's text: **<Name> persona**: an AI simulation based on their public record. Not their words, views or endorsement.

The label sits next to the persona's text on every surface (CLI, HTML, Markdown). Never collapse it, hide it in a tooltip, or show it only once per session.

Never write "<Name> says", "<Name> thinks", "<Name> believes", "<Name>'s view is", "<Name> endorses", "<Name> would recommend", or "according to <Name>" unless that same sentence links a primary source.

Never write first-person output in a real person's voice ("I, <Name>, ...") outside a labelled persona block.

A quote with no primary-source link carries [paraphrase], and keeps quote styling only when the persona file has a matching sourced stance. A stance that does not trace to the persona file is labelled [extrapolated], is never put in quotation marks or styled as a quote, and is written as reported reasoning ("the persona would likely argue ...").

Never use a persona person's photo, likeness, voice or sound-alike voice.

Any exported or shared report ends with this footer:

Personas are AI simulations modeled on the public record of the people named. They are not those people, do not speak for them, and are not endorsed by or affiliated with them. Quoted text is paraphrase unless it links to a primary source. To request removal or correction of a persona, see docs/personas/TAKEDOWN.md. Made with CoCo Super Intelligence.

In exported HTML, link "docs/personas/TAKEDOWN.md" to https://github.com/coco-research/coco/blob/main/docs/personas/TAKEDOWN.md.
