# David Perkins

David Perkins is a cognitive scientist and co-founder of Project Zero at the Harvard Graduate School of Education who has spent five decades investigating the nature of understanding and how to teach it effectively. His central insight challenges the traditional view of knowledge as a stored commodity: understanding is not having information in your head, but being able to do something flexible with what you know. If a student can recite a formula but cannot apply it to a novel problem, they do not understand it — they merely possess inert knowledge.

His "Teaching for Understanding" framework, developed with colleagues at Project Zero, operationalizes this insight into four parts: generative topics (central ideas worth understanding), understanding goals (what students should be able to do), performances of understanding (activities that require applying knowledge in new ways), and ongoing assessment (feedback that improves performance). This framework shifted instructional design from content coverage to cognitive performance, influencing curriculum development worldwide.

Perkins is also renowned for his work on metacognition and creativity. He argues that thinking skills are not mysterious talents but learnable habits of mind that can be made visible and practiced explicitly. His "Visible Thinking" routines — simple protocols like "See-Think-Wonder" or "Claim-Support-Question" — give teachers tools to surface students' cognitive processes in the classroom, making thinking an object of reflection and improvement rather than an invisible black box.

His recent work addresses the crisis of relevance in traditional schooling. In *Future Wise*, he distinguishes between "learnworthy" content (interesting but perhaps not essential) and "lifeworthy" content (knowledge and skills that will matter in students' future lives). He challenges educators to ask not "Is this important?" but "Will this matter in the lives our students will lead?" This question has gained urgency in the AI era, where routine knowledge retrieval is automated, and the premium shifts to judgment, ethics, creativity, and the ability to navigate ambiguity. At 80+, Perkins remains a leading voice urging education to focus on the whole game of life, not just the fragmented drills of school.
