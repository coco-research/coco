Overview

What it is

Available now, v0.11.0

No account, no subscription and no remote API key required. Once the model weights are on disk, dictation works offline at full speed.

All tagged releasesSource repository

Hold a key, speak, and your words are typed into the app you already have open.

Coco Voice is private speech-to-text on your own computer: hold a key to dictate into the app you have open, tidy transcripts with a local model, and keep cited meeting notes on disk with no account and no subscription.

Capabilities

What it can do today

Provably true of the code and releases available today.

01

Push-to-talk in any app, offline

Option+Space on macOS, Ctrl+Space on Windows and Linux, or toggle mode. Recognition works offline once your model is downloaded.

02

Open speech models, SHA-256 checked

Pick your own open speech model, including Whisper, Parakeet and Moonshine. Each download shows its licence and is checked against a pinned SHA-256 before use.

03

On-device clean-up and custom words

A local Qwen3-4B model on Mac tidies text and drops spoken self-corrections such as 'scratch that', alongside custom vocabulary and local history.

04

Meeting notes core with segment citations

Version 0.11.0 stores meeting notes and notepad points locally on disk, with segment citations linking each note back to what was said.

05

Private by design

Dictated text never goes into log files, app data stays private to your user account, the paste is skipped while macOS secure input is on, and if a paste fails the text stays on your clipboard.

06

macOS, Windows and Linux installers

Installers for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows (x64 and ARM64) and Linux (AppImage, deb, rpm on x86_64 and arm64), plus a one-command install on Apple Silicon Macs and 22 UI languages.

Roadmap

What’s cooking

The next milestones sourced from active work and release roadmaps.

01

Dedicated Meetings screens

Dedicated desktop screens for live meeting recording controls, dual-track speaker view, and searchable meeting views.

02

Clean-up on out of the box

Clean-up on out of the box, filler-word removal that follows the language you speak (and can be switched off), and history limits that do what they say.

03

Per-app profiles

Tone, capitals and punctuation follow the app in front, and apps you block are never dictated into.
