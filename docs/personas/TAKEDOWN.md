# Persona removal and opt-out

CoCo Super Intelligence includes AI personas modeled on the public record of public figures: their books, talks, papers, posts and interviews. A persona is not the person. It does not speak for them, and it is not endorsed by or affiliated with them.

**If you are a person with a persona in CoCo,** you can ask us to remove it, correct it, or keep you out of future releases. You do not need to give a reason.

**How to ask:** email **support@cocoresearch.org** with the subject "Persona request". Tell us the persona name, or the link to its file. Please don't open a public GitHub issue for this.

We may ask for reasonable confirmation that the request comes from you or your representative. We only ask for what we need, and we delete it once the request is closed.

**What happens:**

1. Within 5 working days we confirm receipt.
2. The persona is removed from `main` within 10 working days, and from the next release. Your name is added to an opt-out list that our build checks, so it is not re-added unless you later ask us to.
3. Corrections (a wrong role, date or stance) are fixed in the same way, and the change is noted in the release notes without naming you, unless you ask us to.

If you are in the EU, UK or India, you can also use this page to exercise your data-protection rights (for example, objection, correction or erasure). We handle those requests within the time the law requires.

Copies already downloaded by others are outside our control, but every future release honours the removal.
