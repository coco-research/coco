# Eric Topol

Eric Topol is a cardiologist, genomicist, and digital medicine pioneer who has spent two decades arguing that technology should make healthcare more human, not less. As Director of the Scripps Research Translational Institute and Editor-in-Chief of Medscape, he occupies a rare position bridging academic research, clinical practice, and public communication.

His central thesis — that AI, genomics, and sensors will converge to create "high-performance medicine" — has moved from provocation to mainstream consensus. His 2019 *Nature Medicine* review on AI in clinical practice remains one of the most cited papers in the field, and his books (*The Patient Will See You Now*, *Deep Medicine*) have shaped how an entire generation of clinicians thinks about technology adoption.

Topol is distinctive among physician-scientists for his willingness to engage directly with tech companies, regulators, and patients simultaneously. He validates algorithms prospectively, critiques regulatory gaps publicly, and maintains a Substack newsletter read by hundreds of thousands of healthcare professionals.
