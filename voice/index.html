Coco Voice is private dictation and meeting notes, with the speech model running on your own computer.

Hold a key. Say it. It's typed.

Download Coco Voice See the meetings work

For macOS, Windows x64 and Linux x64. Latest release: 0.20.9, 26 November 2026.

Dictation that stays on your computer.

Most dictation tools record you, send the audio to a server and bill you monthly. Coco Voice runs the speech model on your own computer. Hold a key, speak, and the text is pasted into whatever field you are in: a chat, an editor, an email. There is no account and no subscription, and recognition works offline once your model is downloaded.

Push to talk

The default is Option+Space on macOS and Ctrl+Space on Windows and Linux. You can switch to toggle mode.

Your choice of local model

Pick a speech model in onboarding: Whisper, Parakeet, Moonshine and other architectures. Models download once and each keeps its own licence.

Your words, kept

Custom words, whole-word corrections and a transcript history stored in a database on your disk.

Optional clean-up

Clean a transcript afterwards through an OpenAI-compatible API. It runs only when that step is enabled.

Meeting notes, on macOS.

Meetings are built into the macOS builds only, and need macOS 14.4 or later. These are the changes that shipped in the 0.20 releases (0.20.2 to 0.20.9, 25 and 26 November 2026), as listed in the release notes.

Notes

Write them your way

Choose one-shot or pieces for meeting notes. Notes use the template you pick, follow custom template headings, and are written for longer meetings.

Ask

Question your meetings

Ask a question across your meetings and get an answer with citations, from a local SQLite search index. Spoken questions work too, and clicking a source opens the cited meeting.

Find and keep

Search, to-dos, delete

List meetings and search them by any text. Spoken to-dos are pulled out and synced across meetings. Delete a meeting together with its notes and audio.

Also fixed this week: Stop and Pause on a live meeting, starting a new meeting after the last one finishes, and Google Meet being shown as in a call only when the tab shows a meeting code.
