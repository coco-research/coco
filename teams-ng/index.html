Coco Teams NG

A team of AI members you hire, brief and work with in one console.

Who it’s for

People who want to organise AI specialists by role instead of one undifferentiated chat.

Private, in the lab — no public download yet
In the labPrivate

01 Part one

What it does for you

Coco Teams NG is a console for organising AI specialists by role. You hire the members you need, brief them on project work in one place, and review what they produce before it lands.

02 Part two

Who it’s for

People who want to organise AI specialists by role instead of one undifferentiated chat.

You want more than one undifferentiated chat thread: distinct roles with clear scopes, briefs you can inspect, and a calm place to review the results.
