Heliopause by Coco Research

Heliopause by Coco Research is out, as an early preview.

It gives your AI tools a Lead and roles, like a small company, on your own machine.

Heliopause organizes your AI tools like a small company.

Heliopause shows it first.

In your first session, the Lead doesn't hand you a tour. Heliopause gives it one thing it found in your setup to open with, then it asks which project to watch first.

How it runs

Heliopause sends nothing of its own. It runs on your own machine, on localhost.

It runs on 127.0.0.1. No account, no telemetry, no remote sync. Optional GitHub lookups go through your own gh, read-only, and the AI tools you use still talk to their own services.

Setup prints its plan and waits for your yes before it changes anything outside ~/atlas-lab. MIT licence.
