Ruling under test (PLAN.md section 4). Normalise the prompt: trim, lower-case, collapse internal whitespace. It is an approval only when all of the following hold: the prompt contains no question mark anywhere; no negation token ("do not", "don't", "not", "never", "yet") appears as a whole word anywhere in the normalised prompt; and either the prompt with trailing punctuation removed equals one of the phrases exactly, or the prompt starts with a phrase immediately followed by a space, comma, colon, period or exclamation mark. Phrases: approve, approved, approve the plan, proceed, go ahead, ship it, lgtm, yes, proceed. The override form stays "override <gate>: <text>" anchored at the start with a literal colon.

Then five adversarial prompts of your own choosing that a careless matcher would accept and a human would not read as approval; report each with its receipts. Any false positive is BLOCK.
