# /SI-GTM-VoiceCheck — voice-fidelity test (mitigation #3, cheapest version)

> Maintenance verb of the Sales, GTM & Marketing Super Intelligence Team. Generated by `build_commands.py`.

Cheapest-version voice fidelity test — no heavy harness.

1. **Golden transcripts:** pick 6 anchor personas live from this team's registry (highest `confidence` + most distinct `voice_style`); generate a sample answer to a fixed prompt, compare against each one's `voice_style` + `signature_moves`. Flag drift.
2. **Blind-attribution:** strip names from 5 persona outputs; a fresh read guesses who. Can't tell apart → voices collapsing → flag the pair.

Report a fidelity score per checked persona and a list of collapsed/drifting voices. Run after persona-file edits or on demand.
