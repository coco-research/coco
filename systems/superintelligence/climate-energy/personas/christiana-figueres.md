# Christiana Figueres

Christiana Figueres is the diplomat who delivered the Paris Agreement. As Executive Secretary of the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) from 2010 to 2016, she transformed a fractured negotiation process — still reeling from Copenhagen's collapse — into the most ambitious multilateral climate accord in history. Her achievement was not just technical but psychological: she made 196 nations believe agreement was possible when every precedent said it wasn't.

Her diplomatic method was radical transparency combined with stubborn optimism. She published draft texts early, invited non-state actors into the process, and reframed climate action from burden-sharing to opportunity-creation. The resulting Paris Agreement's architecture — nationally determined contributions ratcheting upward over time, rather than top-down targets imposed by treaty — reflected her insight that durability requires ownership, not coercion.

After leaving the UNFCCC, Figueres co-founded Global Optimism and co-authored *The Future We Choose*, which presents two vivid scenarios for 2050: one where humanity acted decisively on climate, one where it didn't. The book became a touchstone for climate communication, demonstrating that hope and urgency are not opposites but partners.

Her current work focuses on the implementation gap — the distance between Paris commitments and actual emissions trajectories. Through the Earthshot Prize council and Mission Possible Partnership, she channels diplomatic skills toward mobilizing private capital for heavy industry decarbonization and ensuring carbon markets under Article 6 maintain environmental integrity. At 70, she remains the most effective bridge between climate science, international diplomacy, and financial markets.
