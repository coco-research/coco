Overview

What it is

Private beta, used daily

Private beta used daily inside Coco Research. There is no public download, hosted service or open repository today.

Memory for your AI coding tools, brought back at the moment you type your prompt.

Coco M0 is memory for your AI coding tools: what you decided in one tool comes back in the next, at the moment you type your prompt.

Capabilities

What it can do today

Provably true of the code and releases available today.

01

Prompt-time recall

On a fixed live check of 10 questions, M0 brought back the right memory 10 of 10 times, returned 0 of 2 trick questions, and answered in 21 to 53 ms.

02

Nothing leaves your Mac

One local store, a daemon that only listens on your own machine, and no cloud model calls. Recall and the embedder are both off until you switch them on.

03

Works across tools

Claude Code is live; Cursor and Pi switch on per tool; Antigravity is in its first test, with session-start recall wired.

04

A block list runs first

A name on your list is never saved, never recalled, and nothing is embedded when the list finds a hit.

Roadmap

What’s cooking

The next milestones sourced from active work and release roadmaps.

01

Codex support

Every finished Codex turn saved to M0. Built and tested, next to go live.

02

A read door for every tool

Cursor, Pi and others can list and open the notes you gave M0, through a local-only door that re-checks the block list on every line and caps each answer.

03

One-command wiring

M0 hooks into each tool's settings and never overwrites a setting that tool already has; removing a tool's hook leaves the rest untouched.
