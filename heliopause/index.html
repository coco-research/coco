Heliopause by Coco Research

Gives your AI tools a Lead and roles, like a small company.

Who it’s for

People running several AI coding tools at once who want to see who is working, idle or blocked.

View source on GitHub Release v0.7.3

Early preview v0.7.3. Apache-2.0.

Early previewApache-2.0 · Open source

01 Part one

What it does for you

Heliopause gives your AI tools a Lead and roles, like a small company, and shows who is working, idle, blocked or waiting on you in a live status page that runs only on your own machine.

02 Part two

Who it’s for

People running several AI coding tools at once who want to see who is working, idle or blocked.

You run several AI coding tools or agents at once and lose track of which one is doing what. You want one local view, with no account and no cloud.

03 Part three

What it does today

True of the code and releases available today.

01

A live map of your agents

Shows each agent as working, idle, blocked or needs-you, with its latest line and pulse links.
