## Step 1 — Read registry (metadata only)

## Step 2 — Score every persona

`total = 0.40*domain_match + 0.30*cell_coverage + 0.30*conflict_pairing`. Greedy pick with re-scoring after each pick. Seed top-5 by domain, then broaden cells, then reinforce conflict pairs.

## Step 3 — Confidence-tiered approval (mitigation #5)

HIGH confidence → 1-line summary + auto-proceed. LOW → AskUserQuestion (approve/edit/re-pick/reject). Hard 16-32 band; out-of-band redline asks confirmation.

## Step 4 — Return approved roster

Emit `APPROVED_ROSTER:` followed by one slug per line for the calling verb to consume. If `--debate` is NOT set, stop here.

## Step 5 — Deliberate (ONLY when `--debate`)

Convene the approved roster as reacting rounds so positions can move. This is the one home for the round protocol — action verbs reference it, they do not redefine it.
- `--debate` (light): you, the running session, voice all personas across the rounds in-context. Cheap, no subagents.
- `--debate=full`: spawn ONE subagent per persona per round (real independence + web research). At 16-32 personas this is many runs — high-stakes only.

1. **Round 1 — independent positions.** Each persona: Position (1-2 sentences) · Reasoning (tied to a cited `public_stance`) · one Open question routed `[for: <slug|anyone>]`. No reacting yet.
2. **Route by productive conflict (NOT all-to-all).** Reuse the `conflict_pairing` you scored in Step 2: each persona reacts ONLY to (a) the on-panel slugs in its own `productive_conflict_with` and (b) anyone who routed a question to it. Never all 16-32 — this bounds the debate and lands the friction where the roster was designed to clash.
3. **React rounds.** Each persona returns: Stance change (`held | sharpened | updated | conceded | hardened`, plus one line on what moved) · Reaction engaging its counterparts BY NAME · Position (now). A concession is a real outcome, not a failure.
4. **Convergence check.** Stop when no persona's stance change is `updated/conceded/hardened` (positions stable) AND no open question another persona could answer remains. Hard cap 4 rounds (Round 1 + up to 3 react rounds); always run at least one react round. State the round count and why you stopped.

Hand the calling verb the converged per-persona positions + a condensed round-by-round transcript. The verb reports genuine splits rather than forcing a winner.
