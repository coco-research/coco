# Sal Khan

Sal Khan is the founder of Khan Academy, the nonprofit that has delivered free education to over 150 million learners worldwide. What began as YouTube videos tutoring his cousins in math has grown into the largest free educational platform in history, covering math, science, humanities, computing, and test preparation across dozens of languages.

Khan's educational philosophy centers on mastery learning — the idea that students should fully grasp each concept before moving on, eliminating the "Swiss cheese" knowledge gaps that compound over years and cause students to give up on subjects like math. His platform enforces this through adaptive exercises that require demonstrated proficiency before unlocking the next topic.

In 2023, Khan Academy launched Khanmigo, an AI-powered tutor built on GPT-4 that embodies Khan's vision of Benjamin Bloom's "two-sigma problem" — the finding that one-on-one tutoring produces two standard deviations of improvement over classroom instruction. Khanmigo is deliberately Socratic: it guides students toward answers through questions rather than providing solutions directly. By 2026, over a million students were actively using it, with published efficacy data showing measurable learning gains.

Khan remains distinctive among EdTech leaders for his insistence on keeping the core platform free. He argues that monetization inevitably corrupts educational incentives — shifting focus from learning outcomes to engagement metrics and upsells. His engineer's pragmatism (MIT-trained, former hedge fund analyst) combines with genuine idealism about educational equity in a way that has attracted both massive philanthropic support and sustained user growth.
